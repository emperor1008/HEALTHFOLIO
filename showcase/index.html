Offline-first · Multilingual · Rural care

Care should not depend on a strong signal.

Healthfolio is an offline-first, multilingual rural-care coordination platform. Patient information is saved locally during connectivity loss and synchronized safely after the network returns — so a dropped signal never becomes a dropped conversation about someone's health.

The problem

Distance is hard. A weak network makes it harder.

In rural areas, the hardest part of healthcare is often not the medicine or the advice — it is everything that has to happen before and between visits.

Long, costly travel

A routine doubt about a report or a medicine can mean a full day of travel to a facility — just to ask one question.

Missed consultations

When every step depends on being physically present, follow-up care is easy to postpone — and hard to resume.

Patchy networks

Connectivity drops without warning. Apps that assume a constant connection simply stop working when they are needed most.

Medicine uncertainty

Without a way to check availability, patients travel to pharmacies on chance — sometimes arriving after a medicine has run out.

How it works

Built for the way connectivity really behaves

Healthfolio treats the network as an unreliable guest — never as a requirement for keeping a record safe.

  1. Save offline

    Records and care requests are saved on the device first, even with no signal. Nothing is lost while you are offline.

  2. Sync safely

    When the network returns, saved work is synchronized automatically — once, securely, and in order. Idempotency keys prevent duplicates.

  3. Route care

    A deterministic safety check helps route each request by urgency. Emergency guidance is never delayed by coordination steps.

  4. Coordinate follow-up

    Clinicians, coordinators, and pharmacies see only what their role permits — assigned requests, consent-shared records, and timestamped availability.

Roles

One platform, four kinds of careful access

Every role is verified and assigned server-side. Each person sees exactly what their role permits — and nothing more.

Patient

Organizes records securely, submits safe care requests, and follows each request's status — even across network dropouts. Personal health information stays private by default.

Doctor

Uses verified, role-based access and views only assigned or consent-shared records. Sets availability honestly, so patients see real options — never invented ones.

Administrator

Manages facilities, verified clinician roles, and operational configuration for their region — without access to patient records or consultations.

Pharmacist

Provides timestamped medicine-availability updates from a real, verified pharmacy account — so patients know before they travel.

Core capabilities

Designed for low bandwidth and high trust

Each capability exists to keep care moving when the network — or the nearest facility — is far away.

Offline queue

On-device persistence with idempotency keys and bounded, exponential retry. Queue items survive refreshes and restarts, and synchronize exactly once.

Multilingual accessibility

English, Hindi, and Odia interfaces with graceful fallback to English — designed for small screens, low literacy, and 44-pixel touch targets.

Safe triage

Deterministic urgency routing with clear escalation guidance. It supports decisions — it never diagnoses, prescribes, or replaces a clinician.

Secure records

Private storage, consent-scoped sharing, short-lived signed URLs, and row-level security enforced in the database — not just the interface.

Care coordination

A validated state machine carries each request from draft to consultation. Every transition is checked and audited on the server.

Pharmacy freshness

Availability is only ever what a pharmacist last confirmed, shown with an honest freshness label — never presented as a real-time claim.

Privacy & clinical safety

Private by design, honest by default

Healthfolio is built for situations where trust matters more than features. Two commitments shape every screen.

Privacy

  • Patient records are private by default; sharing happens only through explicit, revocable consent.
  • Roles are assigned server-side and enforced in the database and on every server route.
  • Pharmacy staff never see patient records, diagnoses, or triage details — only non-sensitive availability requests.
  • Status labels are truthful: nothing is shown as delivered, confirmed, or available until the system itself confirms it.

Clinical safety

  • The triage experience supports urgency routing. It does not diagnose, prescribe, calculate doses, or recommend medicines.
  • Emergency guidance is shown immediately and is never delayed by coordination or matching steps.
  • Availability information is timestamped and attributed — never presented as real-time inventory.
  • Healthfolio complements care; it does not replace a clinician, a pharmacy, or emergency services.

In an emergency, contact your local emergency services immediately.
